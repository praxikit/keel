# Brief

> Fill every section. Short is fine; empty is not. This is the input to the critique.

## What
One paragraph. What is being built, in plain words.

## Who
Who uses it and what they are trying to do. One line per kind of user.

## Why now
What prompted this. What happens if it is not built.

## Done looks like
Three to six observable statements. "A user can …", "The page loads in under …".

## Constraints
Budget (free tier? paid line?), deadline, platforms, devices, languages, must-use or must-not-use
technologies, privacy or legal limits.

## Non-goals
What this explicitly will not do, so nobody adds it quietly.

## Open questions
What we do not know yet and how we will find out.
